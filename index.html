How We Work

Our Trade Process

From sourcing to delivery, every step of our supply chain is designed for transparency, reliability, and uncompromising quality.

01

Source & Verify

We partner with vetted, certified farms and cooperatives, conducting on-site inspections to verify quality and ethical sourcing standards.

02

Test & Grade

All commodities undergo rigorous laboratory testing for moisture, aflatoxin, pesticide residues, and grading before shipment authorization.

03

Document & Ship

We handle all export documentation — Phytosanitary certificates, COA, BOL, and customs clearance — ensuring seamless cross-border logistics.

04

Deliver & Support

Post-delivery support and long-term partnership management ensure your supply chain remains consistent, season after season.

Why Global Buyers
Choose Us

In a market driven by speed and volume, PK International distinguishes itself through relationship-first trade, unmatched transparency, and a relentless commitment to product integrity.

International Certifications

All products comply with ISO 22000, HACCP, FDA, and destination-specific import standards. Full COA and third-party lab reports provided on every shipment.

Secure Trade Terms

We operate under internationally recognized INCOTERMS with flexible payment structures — LC at sight, TT, and DP — designed to protect both buyer and seller.

Full Supply Chain Traceability

From farm origin to port of destination, every lot is traceable. We provide origin certificates, phytosanitary clearances, and fumigation reports as standard.

Reliable Delivery Schedules

Our logistics network spans major ports in West Africa, South Asia, and North America, ensuring on-time shipment with real-time cargo tracking.

Ethical & Sustainable Sourcing

We work exclusively with farming cooperatives that meet fair trade principles and sustainable agricultural practices, supporting farmer livelihoods in origin communities.
